About

I am a PhD student in probability theory under the supervision of Djalil Chafaï and Quentin Cormier. My research focuses on the long-time convergence of diffusions, on abrupt convergence phenomena (cutoff) and on metastability; my thesis is entitled Cutoff and metastability phenomena for ergodic diffusion processes. I am affiliated with Université Paris-Dauphine (CEREMADE), École Normale Supérieure (DMA), Inria Saclay (ASCII) and École Polytechnique (CMAP).
